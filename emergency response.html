RESPONSE PROCESS

Stabilize first. Then understand and recover.

Contain

Limit the spread of the incident and protect critical systems while evidence is preserved.

Investigate

Establish what happened, which systems are affected and what indicators require action.

Remediate

Remove persistence, address exploited weaknesses and restore trusted controls.

Recover

Restore operations safely and strengthen the environment against recurrence.

Document

Capture findings, actions and lessons learned for governance and future prevention.

Harden

Use the incident to improve security posture, monitoring and operational resilience.

DURING AN INCIDENT

Do not make the situation worse.

Where safe and practical, preserve evidence, avoid wiping affected systems, disconnect compromised endpoints from the network when appropriate, and escalate quickly.
